4. Compare the zooming results between Lena and Cameraman. Which image results in higher
PSNR? Which image looks better when restored to the original resolution using digital zoom-
ing methods? Why?
The Lena image results in a higher PSNR over all digital zooming methods. The Lena image looks better when being restored
to the original resolution when comaring method to method. This is because the image size is larger to start with. The
cameraman image is a 256x256 px image and the Lena image is 512x512. When the bilinear reduction of resolution occurs the
images become 64x64 and 128x128 respectively. Before applying digital zooming, the reduced Lena image has 4 times the data
than the reduced cameraman image.

5. What does the PSNR tell you about each of the methods? Does it reflect what is observed
visually?
The PSNR tells us the quality of the restored image with respect to the original image. The images with higher visual quality
have higher PSNR values. With this observation it can be determined that the bicubic zooming method produces an image that is
more similar to the original than the bilinear which is more similar than the nearest neighbour method.
