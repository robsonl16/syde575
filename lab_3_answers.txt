1. What can you say about the general distribution of energy in the Fourier spectra? Why?

Most of the energy is distributed in the middle of the Fourier spectra with some low frequency energy
distributed along the horizontal axis. This is because when we travel across the image horizontally
we see this change in pixel intensity and when we travel across the image vertically there is no change in
pixel intensity which is why there is no energy along the vertical axis in the fourier spectra.
